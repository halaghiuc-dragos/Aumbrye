# Aumbrye Story Bible

## Premise
Aumbrye Tower stands at the edge of a kingdom swallowed by the Umbral Tower — a vertical wound in reality that resets its interior on every failed ascent. The player is the **echo of a fallen warden**: not immortal, but recalled to the tower hub whenever death or escape ends a run.

## Why the tower exists
The Forgotten Castle and linked biomes are memory-fragments of a kingdom that tried to seal the tower. The seal failed; the tower persists, erasing and replaying its depths.

## Why the player loops
An **undying oath** binds wardens who swore at the threshold. Death scatters the echo back to Aumbrye Tower. Successful escapes carry proof outward — loot, XP, and story flags — until the final sovereign is defeated.

## Factions (stub)
- **Tower wardens** — hub NPCs (Elara, Aldric, Mira) who trade, forge, and assign quests.
- **Umbral remnants** — enemies and bosses trapped in the reset loop.
- **Stranded scouts** — rare dungeon NPCs with one-off dialogue.

## Tone
Melancholy dark fantasy with terse NPC voice. Death is expected; persistence is the virtue.

## Ending beat
Defeating the Forgotten Sovereign on the final floor fulfills the oath. The epilogue card acknowledges the echo endures in the hub until the next summons.
