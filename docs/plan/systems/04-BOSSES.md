| Major | Title | Phase |
|-------|-------|-------|
| BOSS-2 | Castle knight slice boss | M2 |

## Boss requirements

- ≥2 phases for theme bosses
- Scripted attacks + arena mechanic
- Clear phase transition signal
- Data-driven where possible; script for unique mechanics
