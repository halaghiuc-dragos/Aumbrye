title: Controls
---

Keyboard and mouse: WASD move, Shift sprint, F jump, Space dodge, left mouse light attack, right mouse heavy attack (hold to charge), Q block (a well-timed press is a just-guard parry), T or middle mouse lock-on, C weapon art, V two-hand toggle, H flask, E interact, 1-4 quick slots (Alt cycles), Tab inventory, K talents, M map, P toggle camera, mouse wheel zoom, Esc pause. In the training arena, R resets the duel.

A gamepad is supported; bindings follow the same actions.
