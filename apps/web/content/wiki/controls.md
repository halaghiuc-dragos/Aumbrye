slug: controls
title: Controls
---

WASD move, mouse look, LMB light attack, RMB heavy attack (hold to charge), Q block (a well-timed press is a just-guard parry), Space dodge, C weapon art, V two-hand toggle, T or middle-click lock-on, E interact, 1-4 quick slots, Alt cycle quick slot, Tab inventory, K talents, Esc pause.
