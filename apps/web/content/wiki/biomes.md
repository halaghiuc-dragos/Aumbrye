title: Biomes
---

The Descent has ten biomes, played in this order: Forgotten Castle, Crystal Caverns, Poison Swamp, Frozen Fortress, Dark Cathedral, Iron Vault, Prism Depths, Venom Mire, Glacial Hollow and Umbral Chapel. Forgotten Castle is open from the start; each later biome unlocks when you clear the one before it. Choose a biome in the menu at the Descent portal in the hub.
